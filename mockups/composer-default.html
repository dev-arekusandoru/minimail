The Sentence — the header is prose you fill in

One clause per job: To who · about what · from which account · then what happens after send. Unset slots stay ghost words; Tab hops clause to clause. Popups, dropdowns and the dropped-recipient note are absolute overlays — nothing under them moves. The footer carries draft state, warning text and Send, nothing else.

A · New message, blurred (default)

The header starts at To. The quiet + at text height adds a recipient; clicking anywhere in the To clause focuses it, and Tab or typing an address starts the same thing. A new message starts its own conversation, so its sentence already reads just send: there is no thread to archive, and the menu leaves that row out. A reply or forward — a draft that continues a thread — reads archive thread.
